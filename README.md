# Kaleb Hoffer

**Operations executive and AI builder in Denver, Colorado.**

I'm Kaleb Hoffer, VP of Strategy & Execution at [Offen Petroleum](https://offenpetro.com), a regulated, multi-state fuel distributor, where I lead the company's AI and technology transformation and write much of it myself.

I treat AI as a practical execution layer rather than a buzzword: automation that removes manual work, connects disconnected systems, and gives teams the visibility to make better decisions faster.

**What I work on**

- **Operations automation:** AP audit, invoice reconciliation, and ERP posting pipelines for high-volume, regulated workflows
- **Decision intelligence:** dashboards and data pipelines that turn fragmented operational data into something leaders can act on
- **Quantitative systems:** event-driven Python services with real-time market data, risk controls, and full audit trails
- **Open research:** reproducible, source-cited evidence engines for public-interest questions

**Selected public work**

- [**NSMPA**](https://github.com/kalebhoffer/nsmpa): an open, reproducible audit of how 1,800 U.S. newsrooms and journalism organizations handle requests to unpublish, de-index, or anonymize old stories. Every finding is quoted verbatim with its source.
- [**OptionDesk**](https://github.com/kalebhoffer/optiondesk): an open-source starting point for building an intraday options trading platform. It covers strategies, risk limits, contract selection, position sizing, exits, paper and Schwab brokers, backtesting and a dashboard, with secrets kept out of code and config.
- [**theta-terminal-client**](https://github.com/kalebhoffer/theta-terminal-client): a small, typed Python client for the Theta Terminal v3 options-data API, with sync and async support. It handles the Terminal's undocumented response formats, status codes and connection failures so trading and research code doesn't have to.
- [**Archive Ethics Project**](https://archiveethics.org): a research initiative on news-archive and de-indexing policy, built on the NSMPA dataset.
